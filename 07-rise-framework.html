Agenda 2026–27

The CSN RISE Framework

Structuring Next-Generation Industrial Competitiveness

  • R — Resilient Infrastructure

    Multi-modal logistics nodes, Samruddhi Expressway feeder networks, and Shendra–Bidkin ring road.

  • I — Industry & IT Innovation

    High-value diversification into ESDM, aerospace, defense components, and green hydrogen systems.

  • S — Skilling & MSME Upgradation

    Scaling supply chains via Central RAMP scheme and dedicated advanced manufacturing tool rooms.

  • E — Ease of Doing Business

    Single-window regulatory clearances, streamlined industrial compliance, and lowered logistics turnaround.
